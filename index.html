About the shop

One shop.
No shortcuts.

Austin grew up in Northern California, always handy and always creative. Working as a bike mechanic instilled the precision that intricate machines demand, but it never fully satisfied his creative side.

A builder and creator at heart, Austin founded White Iron Design to fully embrace that creative genius. Every piece is American made, shaped with craftsmanship, time, and care.

Each table is lovingly handmade from start to finish. A unique work of art, unlike any other.
